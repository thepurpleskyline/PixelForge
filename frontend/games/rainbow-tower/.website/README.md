# 🌈 Rainbow Tower

## Climb the tower · cast your own path

A cute unicorn climbs an endless, rotating tower rising out of a candy sea. There's
no path to the top — you cast rainbows that arc around the tower's surface and land
on them. Bounce on elastics (chain your bounces and each one throws you higher),
stomp the sourbugs, dodge spikes and wisps, and grab ⭐ Rainbow Rush, 🚀 Rocket,
🎒 Jetpack and 🧁 cupcakes on the way up. Height is your score. The sea below never
rises — but touch it once and the run is over.

## Controls

- **Keyboard** — ◄ ► or A/D to run around the tower · ↑/W to jump (double jump in
  the air; keep holding after it to open a rainbow parachute) · **Space** (or V) to
  cast a rainbow · ↓/S to dive while flying · **Esc** to pause.
- **Touch** — drag to steer, tap or hold anywhere to jump and glide, 🌈 button to
  cast, swipe down to dive, ‖ to pause, speaker icon to mute.
- **Gamepad** — stick or d-pad to steer, **A** to jump (hold to glide), **B/X/Y** to
  cast, **Start** to pause. Browsers hide a pad until you press something, so the
  first press just wakes it — the title screen says "gamepad detected", then y
  away.
